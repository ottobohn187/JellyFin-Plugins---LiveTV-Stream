# Music Video Visualizer

Music Video Visualizer adds a rainbow audio spectrum to the bottom of videos
played from administrator-selected Jellyfin libraries.

## Configure

1. Open `Dashboard -> Plugins -> My Plugins -> Music Video Visualizer`.
2. Select `Music Videos` or any other library that should receive the overlay.
3. Adjust the bar count, overlay height, and opacity, then save.

The visualizer is limited to Jellyfin Web. Native television and mobile clients
use separate player interfaces and are not modified by this plugin.
